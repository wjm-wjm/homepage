Jiangming Wang

Researcher, Tencent Youtu Lab

Shanghai, China

I am a researcher at Tencent Youtu Lab, working on computer vision and machine learning. My research interests lie in multimodal large language models, AIGC / Deepfake detection, and video generation & understanding. Previously, I received my M.E. in Computer Science from East China Normal University in 2024, and my B.E. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Zhejiang University of Technology in 2021. I am actively looking for research collaborations. Feel free to reach out via email.

Research Interests

  • Multimodal LLMs — Vision–language understanding, reasoning, and alignment for foundation models.
  • AIGC / Deepfake Detection — Generalizable detection of AI-generated and manipulated visual content across unseen generators and domains.
  • Video Generation & Understanding — Unified multimodal foundation models for video synthesis, temporal reasoning, and world modeling, with an eye on the interplay between generation and forensic detection.
